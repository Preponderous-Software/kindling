Kindling

A homesteader's cabin in the hills has fallen to ruin, and a settler, Ada, is coming to live in it before the first frost. Survey the damage, clear it out, repair it, furnish it to her wishes and light the hearth. The list in the corner says what the step you are on needs.

  • Mouse: look. WASD: walk, Shift: hurry.
  • 1–9: take a tool (` for bare hands). Hold the left button to work with it.
  • E: pick up or put down. R: turn what you carry. L: lantern.
  • F5 / F9: save / load the last save. F1: the controls on screen. Esc: free the mouse.
  • Drag on the left half to walk (all the way out to hurry); drag on the right half to look.
  • Tap a tool along the bottom, then hold Work to use it on what the crosshair is on.
  • Take: pick up or put down. Turn: turn what you carry. Lamp: the lantern.

The game saves every two minutes, when you press Save, and when you leave the page. Saves stay in this browser on this device. Built on the Strata engine; source.

Start again?

This puts the cabin back the way you found it. Your progress here is replaced the next time the game saves.
